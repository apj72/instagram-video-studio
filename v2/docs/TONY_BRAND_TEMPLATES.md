# DJ Tony T video templates

The built-in templates translate `template_maker/DJ Tony T — Brand Guide v2.html` and the companion Template Maker into editable Video Studio projects. The reference folder is ignored by Git; the app uses its own bundled image and font assets.

Open **New from Template** in the editor and choose one of these projects:

| Template | Canvas | Starting content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| September Mix | 1080 × 1350 | September title, featured artists, @TonyT.dj |
| Mix Drop | 1080 × 1350 | Generic new mix announcement |
| Gig Story | 1080 × 1920 | Venue and date placeholders |
| Square Cover | 1080 × 1080 | Animated square mix cover |
| September Mix Motion | 1080 × 1350 | September release with staged directional entrances |
| Gig Story Motion | 1080 × 1920 | Live announcement with a two-part headline |
| Square Cover Motion | 1080 × 1080 | Mix number entering from opposite sides |

The guide's visual order is a lime label, Bungee headline, Permanent Marker tag, and Space Mono details. Portrait projects use a darkened mural or crowd photo, a short spectrum bar, and at most three laser beams. The square cover uses the guide's tie-dye colour treatment. The square canvas is 1080 × 1080 because this is a video template; the guide's 3000 × 3000 cover size applies to still artwork.

The **Add Layer** section has shortcuts for Headline, Lime Label, Marker Tag, and Mono Detail. Select a text layer to edit its words, font, colours, or **Headline Effect**. The effect supports a black outline or stacked pink and orange extrusion. Select **Laser Beams** to change the beam count and glow.

The three **Motion** templates keep each text element as an editable layer. Labels enter from the top, headline parts come from opposite sides, tags rise from the bottom, and details appear in sequence. They hold on screen for reading, then exit near the end. The spectrum bars light up one after another. Open the timeline or Motion panel to adjust the keyframes and timing.

The September video carries short on-screen copy. Use the full mix description, SoundCloud link, and hashtags in the Instagram caption, where they remain readable and clickable. Replace any placeholder details before export.

Bundled fonts: Bungee, Permanent Marker, and Space Mono. License files for the newly added fonts are in `public/assets/fonts/`.
