One frequency.
Five ways to announce it.

A series of 15-second Reels built on the Version1 identity: black, white and one signal of red, over real footage of a laser rig in a dark room. The logo spirals in, slides up to the top and stays; four sections then assemble a piece at a time, hold, and come apart again. Eight bars at 128 BPM, so every cut lands on a downbeat once you drop a mix underneath.

A campaign, not a post

The five designs are meant to be rotated so the feed keeps moving without repeating itself. A suggested run-up to a night:

WhenPostWhy
Three weeks outNight AnnouncementEverything at once: sound, selectors, date, room.
Two weeks outThe SelectorsHands the night to the DJs and their own accounts.
One week outSave the DateQuiet reminder. One date, nothing else to read.
Any weekMix DropKeeps the account about music between nights.
On the dayLast CallDoors, entry and the address, nothing more.

Make the next one

In the video studio open Templates → Version1 Events and pick a design. Every line is a named layer, so you edit the words in place. Add a mix excerpt through the Audio panel, keep the duration at 15 seconds so the cuts stay on the beat, then Save As and export. See the editing guide.

The generic five carry placeholder copy. Replace every date, venue and name before posting.

The background is a graded stock clip, not a Version1 night. To use your own, drop a 1080 × 1920 file into public/assets/version1/ as red-lasers.mp4 — nothing in the templates needs to change. Source, licence and the exact grade.
